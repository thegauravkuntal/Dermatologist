PRP Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy uses your body’s natural growth factors to repair, rejuvenate, and restore skin and hair. By concentrating platelets from your own blood, this treatment stimulates collagen production and accelerates tissue regeneration.
