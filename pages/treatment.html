Botox / Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Anti-wrinkle injections are non-surgical treatments designed to soften fine lines and dynamic wrinkles caused by repeated facial expressions such as smiling, frowning, or squinting. The treatment works by temporarily relaxing targeted facial muscles, helping the skin appear smoother and more refreshed.

✓

Jawline slimming

✓

Neck bands

✓

Excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis)

Will I still look natural?

Yes. At Novae Aesthetics, our approach focuses on subtle, natural-looking results. The aim is to soften lines and refresh your appearance while maintaining natural facial movement and expression.

How long does the treatment take?

Treatment is usually quick and straightforward, taking approximately 20–30 minutes including consultation and assessment.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients describe the treatment as very tolerable. Tiny needles are used, and discomfort is usually minimal and short-lasting.

When will I see results?

Results usually begin to appear within 3–7 days, with full results visible at around 2 weeks after treatment.

How long does Botox last?

Results typically last around 3–4 months, although this can vary depending on individual metabolism, lifestyle, muscle activity, and treatment area.

Is there any downtime?

There is very little to no downtime. Most patients return to normal daily activities immediately after treatment.

What should I avoid after treatment?

Avoid rubbing or touching the treated area, avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours, avoid lying flat for 4 hours, and follow all aftercare advice provided during your appointment.

Is Botox safe?

When performed by qualified medical professionals, anti-wrinkle injections are considered very safe. At Novae Aesthetics, patient safety, anatomy knowledge, and ethical practice are always prioritised.

Who is suitable for treatment?

Anti-wrinkle injections may be suitable for adults wishing to soften lines, prevent deeper wrinkles, or refresh their appearance. A consultation is always required to assess suitability and discuss your goals.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers are injectable treatments designed to restore lost volume, enhance facial features, improve symmetry, and create a more refreshed and youthful appearance. Most fillers used are made from hyaluronic acid — a substance naturally found in the body that helps maintain hydration and structure within the skin.

Facial contouring and balancing

Will my results look natural?

At Novae Aesthetics, natural results are our priority. Our aim is to enhance your existing features in a subtle and balanced way rather than creating an overfilled or artificial appearance.

How long does the treatment take?

Most filler appointments take around 30–60 minutes depending on the treatment area and complexity of the procedure.

Are dermal fillers painful?

Most patients experience only mild discomfort. Many fillers contain local anaesthetic to improve comfort during treatment, and numbing cream may also be used where appropriate.

When will I see results?

Results are visible immediately after treatment, although mild swelling or bruising can occur initially. Final results usually settle within 1–2 weeks.

How long do fillers last?

Longevity varies depending on the product used, treatment area, lifestyle, and individual metabolism. On average, fillers can last anywhere between 6–18 months.

Is there any downtime?

Downtime is usually minimal. Some patients may experience mild swelling, redness, tenderness, or bruising for a few days after treatment.

What should I avoid after treatment?

Avoid excessive touching of the treated area, avoid strenuous exercise for 24–48 hours, avoid alcohol and excessive heat for 24 hours, avoid makeup on the treated area for several hours, and follow all personalised aftercare advice provided during your appointment.

Are dermal fillers safe?

When performed by qualified medical professionals with strong anatomical knowledge, dermal fillers are considered safe. At Novae Aesthetics, patient safety and ethical practice are at the centre of every treatment.

Can fillers be dissolved?

Yes. Most hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved using an enzyme called hyaluronidase if required.

Who is suitable for dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers may be suitable for adults wishing to restore volume, enhance facial features, improve facial balance, or achieve a fresher appearance. A full consultation is required before treatment to assess suitability and discuss expectations.

Skin Boosters

Skin boosters are injectable treatments designed to deeply hydrate the skin, improve skin quality, and restore a healthy, radiant glow. Unlike dermal fillers, skin boosters are not primarily used to add volume or change facial shape. Instead, they work by improving hydration, elasticity, texture, and overall skin health from within.

What concerns can skin boosters help with?

  • Dehydrated or dull skin
  • Fine lines and crepey skin
  • Loss of skin elasticity
  • Uneven skin texture
  • Tired-looking skin
  • Acne scarring
  • Sun-damaged skin
  • Early signs of ageing

  • Acne-prone areas

PRP & PRF Therapy

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) and PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) are regenerative treatments that use your body’s own natural growth factors to support skin rejuvenation and hair restoration.

After a small blood sample is taken, it is processed to concentrate platelets and healing factors before being carefully injected or applied to targeted areas.

These treatments may help improve:

Skin texture and glow
Fine lines and skin quality
Under-eye rejuvenation
Acne scarring
Hair thinning and scalp health

PRF contains a fibrin matrix that allows slower release of growth factors and is commonly used for delicate areas such as under the eyes.

Laser Hair Removal

Our medical-grade laser hair removal treatments are designed to reduce unwanted hair safely and effectively while keeping your skin comfortable throughout the process.

Suitable for both men and women, treatments can be tailored for the face and body including underarms, legs, bikini area, chest, back, beard line, and more.

How It Works

Laser energy targets the pigment within the hair follicle, helping reduce future hair growth while protecting the surrounding skin. Hair grows in cycles, so multiple sessions are needed for best results.

What To Expect

Consultation and patch test before treatment
Mild warmth or snapping sensation during treatment
Minimal downtime
Gradual reduction in hair growth over a series of sessions

Recommended Sessions

Most clients require 6–8 sessions spaced several weeks apart depending on the treatment area, hair type, skin tone, and hormonal factors.
